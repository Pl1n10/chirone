# Zero-shot baselines, step 1 (complaint → category) — 2026-11-08

**What this is:** stock models, no fine-tuning, on the step-1 test split:
531 user messages (282 Italian, 249 English) written by the teacher in the
voice of ten personas each (older people, a shopkeeper, a teenager, a carer
writing in a second language, someone dictating to a phone...). Nine
categories: seven fault families, `actions` (everyday requests such as "open
the calculator", "look up the chemist's opening hours") and `other`.
Output constrained by the JSON-schema grammar, as on the target PC.

Two prompt variants:
- **names only**: the shipped system prompt, which lists no categories (they
  come from the grammar); kept terse because every prompt token costs real
  time on a 2008 CPU;
- **described**: the same prompt plus one line per category with its label.

| Model | names only | described | of which IT / EN (described) | `actions` (described) | invalid JSON |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Qwen3.5-0.8B Q8_0 (llama.cpp) | 34.3% | 41.1% | 35.8% / 47.0% | 11.9% | 0% |
| Qwen3.5-2B Q4_K_M (llama.cpp) | 38.4% | 42.7% | 37.6% / 48.6% | 8.6% | 0% |
| qwen3:14b, reasoning off (Ollama) | 39.2% | 50.7% | 46.1% / 55.8% | 17.7% | 0% |

Commands: `eval/run_eval.py --step 1 [--describe-categories]`; reports in
`eval/report-zeroshot-step1-*.json` (not committed, regenerated by the commands).

## Reading

- **Size barely helps.** From 0.8B to 14B parameters (17×) accuracy moves
  from 34% to 39% with the shipped prompt. The hard part is not language
  understanding but knowing what the catalog's categories mean.
- **Even a 14B model routes everyday requests badly** (18% on `actions`):
  "open the browser" looks like `apps` ("apps that won't open"). That is what
  the catalog-specific fine-tuning has to teach, and why a generic model
  behind a long prompt is not the answer on old hardware.
- **Italian is harder than English for every model**, by 9–11 points: the
  pilot language is the harder one.
- **The grammar works**: zero invalid outputs out of 3,186 answers.

Latency here is the devbox (Haswell, AVX2) and a GPU, not the target: the
old-PC speed proxy is in `2026-11-07-devbox-proxy.md`.

## Caveats

- Test messages are teacher-written (qwen3:14b, Apache-2.0), not real users;
  real complaints from the pilot will replace them as they come.
- The 14B baseline is also the teacher: it saw no test labels, but the
  phrasing style is its own, which flatters it if anything.
